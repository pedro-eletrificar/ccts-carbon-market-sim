Testing round: what to try, what to report

This build has three modes — Solo vs. AI, the Market Simulator, and Live Multiplayer — and none of them have had real users kick the tires yet. This guide is a punch list for that first pass: concrete things to try in each mode, what a working result looks like, and how to log what you find.

Before you start

Skim the How to Play guide if you haven't — it explains the rules this simulator is enforcing, and section 11 ("What's real vs. illustrative") separates known simplifications from actual bugs. If a number looks off, check there first: it might be intentional.

Mode 1Solo vs. AI Operators

Needs: just you, one browser tab

The most-tested mode so far — this pass is about trying to break it, not just playing it straight.

  • Register in a disclosed-target sector (Cement, Aluminium, Chlor-Alkali, or Pulp & Paper) and check the baseline/target numbers look sane.Expect: target intensity below baseline, reduction % roughly matching what the sector tile advertised.
  • Deliberately under-abate (activate 0-1 measures) to force a deficit, then only partially cover it on the exchange.Expect: a penalty at settlement, worked out as 2× the clearing price on the uncovered shortfall.
  • Deliberately over-abate to generate a surplus, offer only part of it for sale.Expect: the unsold portion shows up as "newly banked," and your CCC bank balance carries into Year 2.
  • In Year 1, commit the Deep Process Redesign (the 4th, most expensive measure) and check its status label.Expect: "Pending — effective FY 2027-28" (2-year lead time) — not counted in your intensity yet, but capex was charged immediately.
  • Advance to FY 2026-27 and check which measures from Year 1 have actually landed vs. are still pending.Expect: capex this period only reflects newly committed measures; the 0- and 1-year-lead measures from Year 1 now show "Active," the 2-year one still shows "Pending — effective FY 2027-28."
  • Play through all three years to the final results screen and check the leaderboard and price chart (three points) render with no visual glitches.
  • Register a new company and confirm the game fully resets (no leftover state from the last playthrough).

Mode 2Market Simulator

Needs: just you, one browser tab

This mode never has a single "expected" answer — it's a forecast. What you're checking is that the forecast moves in a sensible direction when you change the inputs.

  • Run once with all default settings, note the price band and compliance rate.
  • Push the Laggard Penalty-Payer strategy weight to maximum, re-run.Expect: compliance rate drops noticeably, and the "penalized" share of the coverage bar grows.
  • Compare a run at Low cost uncertainty + 300 trials against High uncertainty + 10 trials.Expect: the low-uncertainty/high-trial run has a visibly narrower price band (P10-P90) than the high-uncertainty/low-trial one.
  • Push firms-per-sector and trials both to maximum (50 / 300) and confirm it still finishes in a few seconds without freezing the page.
  • Scan the sector breakdown table for anything that looks broken — blank cells, negative percentages, "NaN".

Mode 3Live Multiplayer

Needs: 2+ people, or 2 browser tabs/devices if testing solo

The newest and least-tested mode. This is where finding real problems is most valuable — take your time here.

  • One person creates a room and shares the 4-letter code.Expect: the code is easy to read/say aloud, and shows up immediately on the host's lobby screen.
  • Everyone else joins with that code, each claims a different sector and a starting posture.Expect: seat claims appear on everyone's screen within a couple of seconds, not just the person who claimed it.
  • Try having two people claim the same seat at nearly the same time.Expect: only one of them succeeds; the other gets a clear "already claimed" message, not a silent failure or a broken state.
  • Host starts the game with a few seats still unclaimed.Expect: unclaimed seats are filled by AI operators, visible in the roster with an "AI" tag.
  • Each human submits an abatement plan; host advances before everyone has submitted.Expect: non-submitters default to no new abatement that period — not an error, not a stuck screen.
  • Submit a trading order, then refresh your browser tab mid-game.Expect: you land back in your same seat with your progress intact, not bounced to a blank join screen.
  • Check that everyone sees the same settlement results and leaderboard after the host advances through settlement.
  • Play through all three compliance years to the shared final results screen, then have everyone leave the room.

4. Cross-cutting checks

Worth a quick pass regardless of which mode you're focused on:

  • Toggle your device/browser between light and dark mode mid-session — text should stay readable, nothing should go invisible.
  • Narrow your browser window to phone width (or actually use a phone) — layout should stack, not overflow sideways.
  • If you're comfortable with browser dev tools: open the console (F12 or Cmd+Opt+J) and check for red errors while you play. Screenshot anything you see.

5. Reporting what you find

Log issues on the project's GitHub Issues page: github.com/pedro-eletrificar/ccts-carbon-market-sim/issues. For each one, include:
