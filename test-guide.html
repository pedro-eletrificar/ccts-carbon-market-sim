Testing Guide · All three modes + the facilitator tool

Testing round: what to try, what to report

This build has three modes — Solo vs. AI, the Market Simulator, and Live Multiplayer — plus a Facilitator Dashboard for running several Multiplayer rooms as one training session. This guide is a punch list for a pilot pass by training providers and their members: concrete things to try in each mode, what a working result looks like, and how to log what you find.

Before you start

Skim the How to Play guide if you haven't — it explains the rules this simulator is enforcing, and section 12 ("What's real vs. illustrative") separates known simplifications from actual bugs. If a number looks off, check there first: it might be intentional.

Mode 1Solo vs. AI Operators

Needs: just you, one browser tab

The most-tested mode so far — this pass is about trying to break it, not just playing it straight.

  • Register in a disclosed-target sector (Cement, Aluminium, Chlor-Alkali, or Pulp & Paper) and check the baseline/target numbers look sane.Expect: target intensity below baseline, reduction % roughly matching what the sector tile advertised.
  • On the Target Briefing screen, check the Market Outlook card shows a named scenario, a narrative, and a demand % for all nine sectors.Expect: your own sector's chip is visually highlighted among the nine.
  • On the Abatement Plan screen, drag the production slider to its maximum (130%) with 0 abatement measures active in a soft-demand or flat-demand sector.Expect: the live preview flips to "producing above the demand ceiling," projected revenue color changes, and settlement afterward shows a real, worse-than-1.0x deficit and a larger penalty than the same plan at 100% production would have produced.
  • Deliberately under-abate (activate 0-1 measures) to force a deficit, then only partially cover it on the exchange.Expect: a penalty at settlement, worked out as 2× the clearing price on the uncovered shortfall.
  • Deliberately over-abate to generate a surplus, offer only part of it for sale.Expect: the unsold portion shows up as "newly banked," and your CCC bank balance carries into Year 2.
  • In Year 1, commit the Deep Process Redesign (the 4th, most expensive measure) and check its status label.Expect: "Pending — effective FY 2027-28" (2-year lead time) — not counted in your intensity yet, but capex was charged immediately.
  • Advance to FY 2026-27 and check which measures from Year 1 have actually landed vs. are still pending.Expect: capex this period only reflects newly committed measures; the 0- and 1-year-lead measures from Year 1 now show "Active," the 2-year one still shows "Pending — effective FY 2027-28."
  • Play through all three years to the final results screen and check the leaderboard and price chart (three points) render with no visual glitches.
  • Register a new company and confirm the game fully resets (no leftover state from the last playthrough).
  • Turn on the CBAM module at setup and register in a CBAM-eligible sector (Aluminium, Iron & Steel, Cement, or Fertilizer). On the Abatement Plan screen, drag the EU export-share slider up.Expect: a live-updating "Est. CBAM Cost" figure, and at settlement a matching set of CBAM rows (EU-bound output, embedded emissions, certificates surrendered, CBAM cost) whose numbers line up with what the slider previewed.
  • With CBAM on, toggle Article 9 recognized vs. not recognized at setup and compare the CBAM cost for an identical plan.Expect: "recognized" deducts this year's CCTS clearing price from the EU reference price before charging you; "not recognized" charges the full amount regardless of your CCTS compliance cost.
  • With CBAM on, uncheck "Submit verified emissions data" and compare the cost to the same plan with it checked.Expect: unverified uses a higher (punitive default-value) embedded-emissions figure but skips the flat verification fee — which one is cheaper should depend on your EU export share.
  • Register in a non-CBAM-eligible sector (e.g. Textiles) with the module on.Expect: no CBAM card appears on the Abatement Plan screen, and no CBAM rows appear at settlement — the module only applies to the four eligible sectors.
  • Register with the CBAM module left off (the default) and play a full game.Expect: no CBAM card, no CBAM toggle visible mid-game, no CBAM settlement rows — the game plays identically to before the module existed.
  • On a browser where you've never played before, check the landing page for the guided tour offer, then take it.Expect: five short tip cards appear in order across Setup, Briefing, Abatement, Trading, and Settlement, each dismissible with "Skip." Once finished (or dismissed from the landing page), the offer banner is replaced by a "Replay the guided tour" link in the footnote.
  • Play a full three-year game to the final results screen and check the debrief section below the leaderboard.Expect: a headline profit delta versus a "Cost-Minimizing Complier" counterfactual, a dual-line chart (your actual cumulative profit vs. the counterfactual), and a year-by-year comparison table — all numbers should be finite (no NaN) and the chart's two lines should visibly diverge if your strategy differed from a straight complier.

Mode 2Market Simulator

Needs: just you, one browser tab

This mode never has a single "expected" answer — it's a forecast. What you're checking is that the forecast moves in a sensible direction when you change the inputs.

  • Run once with all default settings, note the price band and compliance rate.
  • Push the Laggard Penalty-Payer strategy weight to maximum, re-run.Expect: compliance rate drops noticeably, and the "penalized" share of the coverage bar grows.
  • Compare a run at Low cost uncertainty + 300 trials against High uncertainty + 10 trials.Expect: the low-uncertainty/high-trial run has a visibly narrower price band (P10-P90) than the high-uncertainty/low-trial one.
  • Push firms-per-sector and trials both to maximum (50 / 300) and confirm it still finishes in a few seconds without freezing the page.
  • Scan the sector breakdown table for anything that looks broken — blank cells, negative percentages, "NaN".
  • Turn on the CBAM module before running.Expect: a new "CBAM: EU border adjustment" card appears in the results with a non-zero average cost per year and a count of EU-exposed operators; re-running with the module off makes that card disappear and shouldn't otherwise change the forecast.

Mode 3Live Multiplayer

Needs: 2+ people, or 2 browser tabs/devices if testing solo

The newest and least-tested mode. This is where finding real problems is most valuable — take your time here.

  • One person creates a room and shares the 4-letter code.Expect: the code is easy to read/say aloud, and shows up immediately on the host's lobby screen.
  • Everyone else joins with that code, each claims a different sector and a starting posture.Expect: seat claims appear on everyone's screen within a couple of seconds, not just the person who claimed it.
  • Try having two people claim the same seat at nearly the same time.Expect: only one of them succeeds; the other gets a clear "already claimed" message, not a silent failure or a broken state.
  • Host starts the game with a few seats still unclaimed.Expect: unclaimed seats are filled by AI operators, visible in the roster with an "AI" tag.
  • Check that the Market Outlook scenario card on the Target Briefing screen shows the exact same scenario name, narrative, and demand percentages on every player's device.Expect: identical scenario for everyone in the room that year — it's drawn once, server-side, not per-client.
  • Each human submits an abatement plan and a production level; host advances before everyone has submitted.Expect: non-submitters default to no new abatement and 100% production that period — not an error, not a stuck screen.
  • Advance to Year 2 and confirm the Market Outlook card shows a different scenario than Year 1 (rerun a few times if it happens to repeat — six scenarios exist, occasional repeats are expected).
  • Submit a trading order, then refresh your browser tab mid-game.Expect: you land back in your same seat with your progress intact, not bounced to a blank join screen.
  • Check that everyone sees the same settlement results and leaderboard after the host advances through settlement.
  • Play through all three compliance years to the shared final results screen, then have everyone leave the room.
  • Before starting, have the host turn on the CBAM module in the lobby.Expect: every player in the lobby — not just the host — sees the module flip to "On" within a couple of seconds, and can see the Article 9 recognized/not-recognized choice once it's set.
  • With CBAM on, a player in a CBAM-eligible seat sets an EU export share and submits, then updates the submission with a different share before the host advances.Expect: the update sticks — settlement reflects the final submitted share, not the first one.
  • With CBAM on, check that a player who never submits an EU export share still settles cleanly.Expect: defaults to 0% EU share (no CBAM cost), not an error or a stuck phase.
  • On the shared final results screen, check that every claimed seat's player sees the same debrief section content (allowing for the profit delta being computed relative to each player's own company).Expect: the counterfactual chart and comparison table render for anyone who held a seat; a spectator (no seat claimed) sees the leaderboard and price chart but no debrief section.

Mode 4Facilitator Dashboard

Needs: just you, one browser tab (a real test also wants a few people to join the rooms)

For running a training session with several breakout groups at once — each group gets its own ordinary Live Multiplayer room, but one facilitator can start and advance all of them from a single screen.

  • From the Live Multiplayer entry screen (Host or Join a Room), find and open the Facilitator Dashboard link.Expect: a small, secondary link below the create/join cards — not a prominent button, since most players will never need it.
  • Create a new cohort with a session label and 3+ rooms.Expect: a facilitator code (format F-XXXXX) and a facilitator token are both shown, plus one 4-letter room code per room, all still in Lobby.
  • Share one room's 4-letter code with someone and have them join it from the ordinary Live Multiplayer join screen.Expect: joining a facilitator-created room works exactly like joining any other room — no special steps.
  • Click Start All Rooms.Expect: every room in the cohort moves from Lobby to Briefing, visible on the dashboard within a couple of seconds.
  • Click Advance All Rooms a few times in a row.Expect: every room advances through Abatement → Exchange → Settlement together, with unclaimed seats auto-filled by AI exactly as in an ordinary room.
  • Use the per-room Advance button on just one room instead of the bulk button.Expect: only that room advances — the others stay where they were, so rooms can be at different phases at the same time.
  • Before starting, toggle the CBAM module on for the whole cohort.Expect: every room in the cohort picks up the CBAM setting — confirm by opening one room's join screen and checking the lobby's CBAM card.
  • Reload the page, then use Reconnect to a cohort with the facilitator code and token you were given.Expect: the dashboard comes back exactly as you left it. Also try the "Reconnect to last cohort" shortcut, which should appear automatically if you reconnected or created a cohort in this browser before.
  • Try reconnecting with the right code but a wrong token.Expect: the dashboard still loads (read access needs no token), but clicking Start All or Advance All shows a clear "Not authorized" error instead of silently failing.

5. Cross-cutting checks

Worth a quick pass regardless of which mode you're focused on:

  • Toggle your device/browser between light and dark mode mid-session — text should stay readable, nothing should go invisible.
  • Narrow your browser window to phone width (or actually use a phone) — layout should stack, not overflow sideways.
  • If you're comfortable with browser dev tools: open the console (F12 or Cmd+Opt+J) and check for red errors while you play. Screenshot anything you see.
